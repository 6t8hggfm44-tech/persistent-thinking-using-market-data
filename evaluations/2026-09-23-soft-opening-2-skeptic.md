## Strongest counter-case to H003

H003 narrowly leads H001 and concerns fiscal pressure, inflation and long yields. The strongest retained alternative is mitigation with continued disinflation. The prior Market state records a reduced-rate East-West pipeline restart; lagged U.S. petroleum product builds and gas stocks above their five-year comparison oppose a uniform scarcity account. Slower annual wage pressure also leaves a benign inflation path plausible.

These observations remain bounded. Conditional Hormuz reopening is not observed reopening. U.S. petroleum periods ending September 11 do not establish that later global disruption was resolved. No new report directly measures September consumer-price pass-through.

Resilient selected credit, labor and industry measures mainly weaken immediate H002 contraction. Nominal retail and earnings strength can fit both H001 and H003. Without aligned prices, incomes, long yields and credible controls, those findings do not choose between them. The Fiscal report does not measure fiscal impulse, Treasury duration supply or real policy-induced construction. Corporate findings do not establish broad productivity diffusion under H004.

## Frozen discriminator and falsifiers

P000029 remains unchanged: **66%** probability that first-release September headline CPI-U, seasonally adjusted, rises at least **0.4% month over month**; point **0.5%**; 80% interval **0.1% to 0.9%**. It resolves at the October 14, 2026 BLS release.

This forecast tests near-term measured pass-through. A favorable binary result alone cannot validate persistent inflation, fiscal impulse or duration-supply causation. Score the probability, point, interval and frozen benchmarks separately. A result of 0.4% would satisfy the event while the model's point forecast loses to the frozen 0.4% no-change benchmark.

The existing surprising conditions remain in force: headline CPI at or below 0.1% despite sustained retail-fuel pressure would weaken near-term pass-through calibration; a result at or above 0.8% with broad acceleration would indicate stronger transmission than assumed. For broader H003, repeated soft inflation and lower long yields alongside resilient activity would favor H001. Fiscal or duration expansion without the predicted rate or inflation response would weaken that mechanism.

H002 would require prospective, persistent real-demand or labor weakness followed by wider credit, rising layoffs or broad earnings deterioration. H004 requires realized output-per-input improvement and diffusion beyond selected firms. These are future discriminators, not newly launched collections or forecasts.

No new resolved outcome is supplied here. Replays do not increase the existing sample of 22 resolved probability forecasts or support a learning claim. Statistical validation remains separate from this semantic review.
