## E1 — July new-home sales weaken materially

**Available:** 2026-08-25 10:00 ET  
**Source:** U.S. Census Bureau / HUD, https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/inventory.html ; Reuters cross-check, https://www.reuters.com/world/us/new-us-single-family-home-sales-slide-july-2026-08-25/

**OBSERVATION:** New single-family home sales fell **10.5% m/m** in July to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of **607,000**, below the Reuters-polled 620,000 consensus and the lowest pace since January. June was revised sharply upward to **678,000** from the previously reported 628,000, underscoring the series' revision/volatility risk. The median new-home price was **$393,800**, the lowest in four years according to Reuters.

**INFERENCE:** This is fresh confirmation that housing remains a weak household-sensitive channel under high mortgage rates. It raises H002's delayed household-downturn branch modestly, but the large prior-month revision and known monthly volatility argue against treating one print as evidence of generalized contraction.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: adverse at the margin; a soft landing can include weak housing, but repeated rate-sensitive weakness is a constraint.
- H002: supportive, especially as confirmation of the existing household/housing branch.
- H003: compatible because persistently high long/mortgage rates are an explicit transmission channel, but the sales decline itself does not identify why rates are high.
- H004: little direct information.

## E2 — Consumer confidence weakens in expectations, while current labor perceptions improve

**Available:** 2026-08-25 10:00 ET  
**Source:** The Conference Board, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-consumer-confidence-edged-down-slightly-in-august-302859371.html ; Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/business/us-consumer-confidence-falls-august-conference-board-says-2026-08-25/

**OBSERVATION:** The Conference Board Consumer Confidence Index fell to **89.4** in August from a revised **90.2** in July. The **Present Situation Index rose 6.8 points to 121.2**, while the **Expectations Index fell 5.8 points to 68.2**. The jobs differential improved to **+7.5%** as more respondents said jobs were plentiful and fewer said jobs were hard to get. Reuters reported 12-month consumer inflation expectations rising to **5.8% from 5.6%**, while home-purchase intentions fell materially.

**INFERENCE:** The split reinforces the model's bifurcation rather than a simple recession signal: current labor perceptions improved while future business/labor/income expectations deteriorated. The weaker expectations and homebuying intentions modestly raise H002's future-demand risk; the rise in inflation expectations modestly supports H003's expectations/policy-constraint branch. The confidence headline is a survey proxy, not a direct activity measure, so it is not used as a sufficient statistic for consumption or labor demand.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: mixed/adverse; stronger current labor perceptions help, but deteriorating expectations and household plans are a warning.
- H002: modestly supportive through expected future demand/labor weakness, not through current layoffs.
- H003: modestly supportive through higher inflation expectations despite weaker confidence.
- H004: little direct information.

## E3 — Oil drops sharply despite continuing physical/geopolitical risk

**Available:** 2026-08-25 after U.S. settlement  
**Source:** Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-prices-steady-investors-weigh-impact-expanded-us-sanctions-against-iran-2026-08-25/

**OBSERVATION:** Brent settled down **3.9% at $88.58** and WTI down **3.1% at $82.36**, both at roughly one-week lows. Reuters attributed the decline primarily to the market viewing the latest sanctions/economic-pressure campaign as less threatening to immediate oil supply than military escalation and to renewed hopes for negotiation. Physical risk remains nontrivial: commodity-vessel transits through the Strait of Hormuz remained extremely low and a tanker was struck near Oman.

**INFERENCE:** This is meaningful contrary evidence to H003's **immediate energy-escalation** branch. It does not eliminate the energy tail because physical constraints remain, but the model should not extrapolate elevated oil mechanically upward. The fall modestly eases the near-term real-income/inflation squeeze and helps H001 relative to H003 on that channel alone.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: modestly supportive through lower immediate energy pressure.
- H002: mixed; the move appears primarily geopolitical/supply-expectation driven rather than a clean demand-collapse signal.
- H003: adverse to the near-term oil-escalation branch; not a falsification of fiscal/private-duration or already-embedded inflation-persistence branches.
- H004: little direct information.

## E4 — Fed Collins keeps a conditional tightening path live

**Available:** 2026-08-25 before cutoff  
**Source:** Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/business/us-rates-need-rise-soon-absent-evidence-ongoing-drop-inflation-feds-collins-says-2026-08-25/

**OBSERVATION:** Boston Fed President Susan Collins said policy would likely need to tighten soon **if evidence of sustained inflation progress does not materialize**, while still describing gradual disinflation as her base case. Reuters noted the federal funds target remains 3.50%-3.75% and that upcoming core PCE remains well above the 2% target on a year-over-year basis.

**INFERENCE:** This is modest support for H003's policy-constraint branch, but it is conditional guidance from one policymaker, not a policy decision or proof of a hike. It partly offsets the inference from lower spot oil.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: compatible only if forthcoming inflation data continue to improve enough to keep the conditional hike path from being activated.
- H002: mixed; tighter policy would raise later downside risk, but the comment is not evidence of current contraction.
- H003: supportive of a binding inflation/reaction-function constraint.
- H004: little direct information.

## Combined inference

The Aug. 25 evidence strengthens the **household/housing weakness** branch without showing a current generalized recession: new-home sales weakened sharply and forward consumer expectations deteriorated, but current labor perceptions improved. H003 receives offsetting signals: oil fell materially, weakening the immediate energy squeeze, while consumer inflation expectations rose and a Fed policymaker kept near-term tightening conditionality explicit. The net update is deliberately small: shift 0.01 from H001 to H002 while leaving H003 unchanged and narrowly leading. No new causal mechanism is added; this cycle reinforces existing housing/household, expectations, energy, and policy-reaction channels.
