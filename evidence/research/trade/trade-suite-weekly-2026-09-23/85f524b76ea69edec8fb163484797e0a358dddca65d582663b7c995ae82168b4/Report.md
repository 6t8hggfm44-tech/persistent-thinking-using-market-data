# Trade: loaded-container weakness masked by empty flows

## Summary

OBSERVATION — In the retained Port of Los Angeles table, August 2026 loaded imports were **0.84% lower** than August 2025 and loaded exports **9.28% lower**. Combined loaded traffic fell **2.54%**, while empty containers increased **4.16%**. Total traffic fell just **0.26%** because the increase in empties offset **84.72%** of the loaded decline.

INFERENCE — Nearly unchanged aggregate TEUs do not demonstrate unchanged cargo-bearing traffic at this port. The result supports a local loaded/empty-composition distinction, not a claim of broad global-trade contraction. TEUs measure container capacity, not goods value or weight.

This September 23 analysis reuses ten reviewed cells retrieved September 22. **It contains no new publisher observations** and is not independent confirmation of the earlier manual reports using these same cells. No current China, Korea, Taiwan or European numerical finding is claimed. Data attribution: [Port of Los Angeles, container statistics](https://portoflosangeles.org/business/statistics/container-statistics).

## Hypotheses and tests

Selection preceded the current calculations. Three leads were ranked: local container composition, Asian semiconductor breadth, and China real activity. Local composition was selected because exact, compatible retained observations supported discriminating accounting tests. The others lacked required numerical inputs. This is exploratory retrospective analysis, not a blinded or prospective forecast. General project history was already known; earlier Trade conclusions were not consulted before freezing this narrative.

H1: loaded inbound and outbound local traffic moved in the same direction. Opposite signs would falsify that description. **Supported locally:** both declined. The much larger export percentage decline does not establish a national export decline or a calibrated anomaly.

H2: total-container changes cannot be interpreted without the loaded/empty split. **Supported:** total loaded fell 16,030.25 TEUs, whereas empties rose 13,581.60. Imports and exports contributed −0.4396 and −1.2331 percentage points to total growth; empties contributed +1.4172 points, summing to −0.2555%. Loaded traffic's share fell from 65.94% to 64.43%, a **1.51-percentage-point** decline. Empty repositioning partly offsets the loaded contraction rather than explaining it away.

T1 executed matched-month percentage and absolute changes. T2 verified imports plus exports equal loaded, and loaded plus empties equal total, with zero residual in each year. T4 independently parsed display strings using exact rational arithmetic and checked the Decimal calculations, offset ratio and component contributions. These are computational checks on one source, not multiple independent measurements.

T3, the monthly-versus-YTD or multimonth sensitivity, is **blocked**: no YTD or intermediate observations were retained. No absent values were reconstructed. The two other domain recipes—China real-activity breadth and Korea/Taiwan semiconductor breadth—are **blocked** by missing compatible quantities, working-day adjustments, prices and product/destination denominators.

H3: the local change represents wider demand rather than port substitution, front-loading or operational timing. **Unresolved.** T5 lacks matched alternative-port flows and customs volumes. The single-port evidence cannot discriminate demand from redistribution. Contradictory comparator data or a documented shift between ports could overturn any broad-demand interpretation, which is therefore withheld.

## Limitations

The dominant contrary fact is rising empty traffic: aggregate TEUs are almost flat even as both loaded categories fall. Calendar-month matching mitigates one seasonal mismatch but does not adjust holidays, shipment timing, tariff anticipation, congestion, port labor/capacity, commodity mix or trade routes. Two observations establish a year-on-year difference, not persistence, statistical significance or an unusual historical tail.

ASSUMPTION — Retained semantic review correctly identifies the publisher's monthly row and column labels. Exact file/hash verification cannot independently establish original publisher bytes, table completeness or revisions. The source is one port and one terminal-count ancestry; repeating it in multiple reports adds no evidence weight.

INFERENCE — Local container handling exposure changed in composition and loaded throughput. Effects on trucking, rail, warehouses or carrier revenue require compatible operating and rate data. SPECULATION — Broader consumption weakness, tariff front-loading or route substitution could contribute, but none is established here; no earnings, inflation, asset-price or causal market effect is inferred.
