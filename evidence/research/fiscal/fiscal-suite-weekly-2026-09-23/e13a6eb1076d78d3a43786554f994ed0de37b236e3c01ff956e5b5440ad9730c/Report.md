## Hypotheses and tests

The plan was registered before this run's recalculation. Previous Fiscal results had already been seen, so the work is **exploratory reuse**, not blinded rediscovery. No other domain's report was read during this independent investigation. Five leads were considered: cash flow, procurement, infrastructure, policy effects, and state/local finances. Construction was selected because it alone had usable retained numeric evidence, not because of a desired market conclusion.

- **H1: Public nominal construction is broadly accelerating.** A positive monthly and annual comparison would be consistent with this limited description. A nonpositive comparison or a directionally inconclusive interval weakens it. The monthly point decline alongside annual growth weakens the broad claim; the published interval prevents a firm monthly directional finding.
- **H2: Prices, composition, normal variation or revisions explain the nominal pattern.** These alternatives remain plausible. Deflators, revision histories, additional months and compatible project controls are missing, so this run cannot separate them.
- **H3: Federal infrastructure policy increases real work performed.** This requires funded-project exposure, price adjustment, pretrends and valid controls. Those inputs are absent; the causal claim is untested.
