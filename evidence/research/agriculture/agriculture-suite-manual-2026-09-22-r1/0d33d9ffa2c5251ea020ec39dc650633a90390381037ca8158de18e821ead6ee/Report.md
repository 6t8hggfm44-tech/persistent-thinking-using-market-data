# Agriculture: supply attribution remains untested

## Summary

The reviewed sources identify relevant releases and geographically mixed drought conditions, but they do not establish a crop, livestock or commodity-balance loss. This is a final evidence-gap finding with an executed source-quality check, not evidence that agriculture was unaffected.

## Coverage

Four official/public sources were checked on September 22. WASDE identified September 11 and August releases; NASS identified the September crop-production briefing and report directory. Neither supplied balance or crop-outcome tables. APHIS supplied detection links, without dated animal counts or production data. The Drought Monitor described conditions valid September 15, released September 17. Three point-well comparisons are ancillary quality checks, not mainland crop measurements.

## Hypotheses and tests

Four leads were assessed against the original plan: crop-weather loss, commodity-balance tightening, animal-disease supply loss, and fertilizer/trade constraints. Crop-weather attribution lacks crop-stage acreage, irrigation, matched yields and independent exposure controls. WASDE balance attribution lacks beginning stocks, production, imports and use. Disease attribution lacks deduplicated detections, removals and output; increased surveillance remains an alternative. Fertilizer and shipment inputs are outside the enabled measurements. All four causal tests remain blocked.

The drought narrative reports deterioration in much of Texas/Oklahoma but improvement in parts of Iowa, Kansas and Nebraska. Uniform exposure cannot be assumed. From the paired well depths, the computed year-over-year deepening is 7.23 feet at St Croix, 6.05 at St John and 0.19 at St Thomas; St Thomas improved 1.35 feet from the previous week. St John's narrative states 6.10 feet, differing by 0.05 from 18.73−12.68. That exceeds a conservative 0.015-foot combined rounding bound. The discrepancy is flagged, with both inputs preserved; no crop-loss estimate is derived from these wells.

## Limitations

Drought categories, point groundwater and detection announcements are proxies or discovery evidence. They cannot identify crop output, national food availability or price transmission. Existing source cooldowns blocked further same-source acquisition; linked report destinations also require their own permitted URL scope. The disabled export-sales source was not enabled. Missing compatible tables remain missing, not zero. Selection and the ancillary arithmetic check are exploratory.
