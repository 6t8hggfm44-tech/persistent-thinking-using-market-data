## Hypotheses and tests

The initial exploratory plan, recorded before this invocation's calculations, compared three explanations for a changing corn balance: **H1 production revision**, **H2 demand revision**, and **H3 beginning-stock or accounting revision**. It required beginning stocks, production, imports, domestic use, exports, total use and ending stocks in each of two like-for-like vintages. A missing value was never replaced by zero. The conversation and procedures had already disclosed the earlier data gap; this is not a blinded selection.

**T1, balance and stocks-to-use: blocked.** Direct inspection found zero retained WASDE numerical cells. Two vintages times seven required fields leaves 14 missing inputs. Neither supply-minus-use identities nor ending stocks divided by total use can be calculated. The deterministic balance calculator was deliberately not fed synthetic or incompatible values. An index heading is not a balance table. There is no basis to prefer H1, H2 or H3.

**T2, crop/weather attribution: blocked.** No compatible crop-stage acreage weights, exposed/control areas, NASS outcome series or irrigation controls exist in the retained set. Well depth cannot substitute for crop-water exposure. A weather explanation would weaken if matched controls deteriorated similarly or exposure missed the vulnerable stage; neither falsifier can be evaluated here.

**T3, disease/output and fertilizer/trade transmission: blocked.** Detection navigation supplies neither dated incident counts nor realized production loss. Surveillance intensity, seasonal herd cycles, feed prices, replacement lags and unaffected regions remain uncontrolled. Fertilizer quantities, application timing, shipment fulfillment and inventory buffers are also missing. Sales commitments, if later obtained, must not be treated as completed shipments.

**D1, retained measurement-quality audit: completed, not an economic hypothesis test.** After inspecting the numerical selection, an explicitly exploratory amendment registered point-depth subtraction and a separate rational-arithmetic cross-check. Current minus year-ago depths below land surface are **+7.23 feet at St Croix**, **+6.05 feet at St John**, and **+0.19 feet at St Thomas**. Positive values mean deeper water below the surface, not a higher water level. St Thomas current minus the retained prior-week value is **−1.35 feet**; the prior-week exact date is not established by its cell. This differing weekly direction cautions against inferring a uniform trend.

St John's selected endpoints, 18.73 and 12.68 feet, subtract to **6.05**, while the retained narrative difference is **6.10**: an unresolved **0.05-foot discrepancy**. The audit does not choose which source statement should be corrected. Decimal subtraction and independently parsed exact rational arithmetic agree on the endpoint calculation. They are two calculation methods on the same retained evidence, not independent observations or publisher validation.
