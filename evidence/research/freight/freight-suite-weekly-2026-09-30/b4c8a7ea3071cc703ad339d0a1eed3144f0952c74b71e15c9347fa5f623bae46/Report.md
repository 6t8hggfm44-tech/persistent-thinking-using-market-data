## Interpretation and contrary explanations

**ASSUMPTION:** a same-calendar-month port comparison is a useful descriptive baseline. It does not eliminate working-day differences, inventory timing, routing changes, cargo mix or base effects.

**SPECULATION, not a finding:** local loaded weakness could reflect demand, diversion to other ports or changed shipment timing. Empty movements could reflect repositioning. Distinguishing those explanations requires independently observed controls and defensible transport lags. Neither movement counts nor aircraft tracks establish cargo tonnage, carrier margins, consumer spending or inflation. A rate increase alone would not identify a capacity bottleneck.
