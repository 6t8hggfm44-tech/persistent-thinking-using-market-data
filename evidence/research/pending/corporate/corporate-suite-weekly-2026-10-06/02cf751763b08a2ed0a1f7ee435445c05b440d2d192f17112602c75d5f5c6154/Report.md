## Hypotheses and tests

### H1 — BEA revision robustness

**OBSERVATION:** The retained August 26 vintage reported current-production profits with IVA/CCAdj of $4,827.4 billion for 2026 Q2 and $4,426.5 billion for Q1. The retained September 30 vintage revised those levels to $4,709.5 billion and $4,325.4 billion. Using displayed values, the quarter-to-quarter change moved from $400.9 billion (9.0568%) to $384.1 billion (8.8801%). Q2 was revised down $117.9 billion, Q1 down $101.1 billion, and the displayed change down $16.8 billion, or 0.1767 percentage point.

**INFERENCE — supported with limits:** The positive direction survives the release-vintage sensitivity, so the revision does not falsify the prior directional observation. It does weaken the magnitude. Rounded displayed subtraction can differ from an official change computed using unrounded data, and two aggregate quarters do not establish a durable trend, sector breadth or issuer-GAAP behavior.

### H2 — single-issuer operating control

**OBSERVATION:** For Microsoft's quarters ended June 30, retained cells show revenue of $90,007 million in 2026 versus $76,441 million in 2025, a 17.7470% increase. Operating income rose 18.2968%. Operating margin increased 0.2096 percentage point while gross margin fell 1.3879 points. GAAP net income rose 31.3333%; the issuer-adjusted comparison rose 22.4868%. Revenue minus cost of revenue equals retained gross margin in both quarters.

**INFERENCE — supported for the issuer only:** Revenue and operating-income improvement survive arithmetic controls, but mixed margins and the GAAP/adjusted gap reject a simple one-number characterization. One issuer cannot establish broad corporate investment or capital-allocation behavior, and cash capex, leases, acquisitions and executed repurchases were unavailable.

### H3 — labor realization boundary

**OBSERVATION:** Retained July versus revised June JOLTS cells imply +0.1 million openings, −0.2 million hires, −0.2 million separations, −0.1 million quits and −0.1 million layoffs/discharges; rounded July hires minus separations is zero. The verified checkpoint contains no matched issuer-event ledger with announcement/effective dates, denominators and realized outcomes.

**INFERENCE — inconclusive for issuer behavior:** These aggregate, stale flows cannot verify company hiring freezes or realized layoffs. Rounded flows do not establish exact payroll change or statistical significance.

**ASSUMPTION:** The retained limited extracts preserve the semantic meaning recorded during their original reviews. Their exact checkpoint hashes were verified, but their original publisher bodies were not available for a new audit.

**SPECULATION:** No current market consequence is scored. No other domain's current report was read before these findings were frozen; later reconciliation must count shared BEA, Census and BLS ancestry once.

Blocked tests were cash investment, executed repurchases, inventory/working-capital conversion, guidance revisions, completed M&A and cash restructuring outcomes. Missing evidence was not coded as zero.
