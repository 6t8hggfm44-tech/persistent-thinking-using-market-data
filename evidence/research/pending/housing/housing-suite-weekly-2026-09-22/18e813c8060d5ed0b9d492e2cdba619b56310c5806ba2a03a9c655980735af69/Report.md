## Hypotheses and tests

Four domain leads were assessed. Financing/demand, construction pipeline and inventory absorption were prioritized because their catalogued primary measures could directly address the mandate. CRE remained a coverage lead because realized property or credit outcomes were absent. **No lead passed the measurement gate**, so no confirmatory case was selected. The exploratory plan disclosed prior awareness of source availability; no unseen economic observations were treated as preregistered discoveries.

1. **Financing versus demand — untested.** Higher financing costs could weaken purchase demand and later sales; seasonality, borrower mix, rate lock-in, weather or revisions could also explain changes. The planned comparison requires product-specific weekly mortgage rates and at least 36 monthly observations where available, with three-month means versus preceding three months and year-ago periods, 0–3-month lags and seasonal/mix controls. No matching inputs were present. There is no claim that rates rose or demand fell.
2. **Demand slowdown versus completion bottlenecks — untested.** Weak demand predicts weaker permits followed by starts; labor/material bottlenecks could lengthen a pipeline despite sustained permits. Normal project timing and multifamily or regional composition are alternatives. Compatible permits, starts, completions, stocks, payroll/hours and materials evidence were missing. No backlog ratio or construction-duration inference was computed.
3. **Weak absorption versus normal supply completion — untested.** Inventory can rise because sales weaken or because completions add supply. Matching inventory and sales denominators, construction-stage mix and seasonal controls are required to distinguish these explanations. No months-supply decomposition was possible. Neither a median-price change nor a market-wide price-cut claim was inferred.
4. **CRE stress transmission — untested.** Reported lending caution alone would not prove realized losses or construction effects. The comparison needs aligned property types, locations, refinancing windows and actual loan/property outcomes. None were available. No bank-opinion direction or realized transmission is claimed.

The completed computational work was an **offline evidence-availability audit**, not an economic test.
