# Global trade and China — October 7, 2026

## Summary

**Final evidence-gap finding:** no verified fresh numerical trade measurement was obtained in this bounded run, so there is no basis for declaring a new global trade turning point. Fresh official Taiwan Ministry of Finance metadata confirms that a monthly exports-and-imports book was listed with a September 23 publication date, but the returned index contained no trade values, commodity detail, quantity indices or observation-month identification.

**OBSERVATION:** retained Port of Los Angeles data for August 2026 still show loaded imports down 0.84%, loaded exports down 9.28%, and total loaded containers down 2.54% from August 2025, while empties rose 4.16% and total containers slipped only 0.26%. **INFERENCE:** at this port and in that month, the headline total masked weaker loaded activity. This is a recomputation of previously reviewed observations, not a fresh release or independent confirmation.

## Hypotheses and tests

**H1 — August loaded activity at Port LA was softer than a year earlier. Supported descriptively, with no new evidence weight.** T1 recomputed five changes from exact decimal cells and matched each published rounded rate. T2 reconciled imports plus exports to loaded flows and loaded plus empty flows to total flows with zero residual. Higher empties offset 84.72% of the loaded decline, and loaded share fell 1.51 percentage points.

**H2 — The local port pattern establishes a broad global-demand contraction. Unresolved.** The required comparator-port, customs-quantity, pre-trend, cargo-mix and timing controls were unavailable. Route substitution, front-loading, operating conditions and composition remain live alternatives. A one-port TEU result cannot identify global quantities or final demand.

**H3 — Asian export strength reflects broad quantity demand rather than semiconductor price or product mix. Untested.** The fresh Taiwan index confirms only that a monthly publication exists. It does not provide compatible Taiwan or Korean completed-month values, chip/non-chip breadth, working-day adjustments, currency controls, quantum indices or unit values.

**China and commodities:** physical production, property, energy and commodity customs quantities were not obtained in compatible current form. Their absence is a measurement gap, not evidence of stability or deterioration.

## Interpretation and competing explanations

**ASSUMPTION:** matched calendar months offer a useful descriptive baseline. They do not control for working days, inventory timing, tariff implementation, route changes, cargo mix or local operating conditions.

Loaded-container weakness could arise from demand, timing or displacement; empty growth can reflect equipment repositioning. The arithmetic supports a local composition statement only. **SPECULATION, not adopted:** tariff front-loading or route substitution may have contributed. Neither mechanism was measured here. No causal, market-price, company-revenue or trading conclusion follows.
