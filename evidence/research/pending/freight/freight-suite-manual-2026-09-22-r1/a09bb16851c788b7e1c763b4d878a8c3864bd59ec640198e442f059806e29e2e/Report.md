# Freight: container composition limits the headline

## Summary

August 2026 Port of Los Angeles total container activity was nearly flat year over year, but loaded activity fell. Empty-container growth offset most of that decline. This supports a local composition explanation; broad goods-demand weakness and rerouting remain untested.

## Coverage

This September 22 manual investigation uses five paired August 2026/2025 TEU measures from one port. It also reviewed BTS, AAR and USDA publication pages. Those pages did not supply the compatible numerical tables required for the broader tests. Observation months are not a new weekly series.

## Hypotheses and tests

| Measure | August 2026 TEU | August 2025 TEU | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Loaded imports | 500,301.50 | 504,514.25 | −0.835% |
| Loaded exports | 115,561.25 | 127,378.75 | −9.277% |
| Total loaded | 615,862.75 | 631,893.00 | −2.537% |
| Empty | 340,043.75 | 326,462.15 | +4.160% |
| Total | 955,906.50 | 958,355.15 | −0.256% |

Loaded share fell 1.508 percentage points. The 13,581.60-TEU empty increase offset 84.725% of the 16,030.25-TEU loaded decline. Component sums reconcile exactly; independent hundredth-TEU and decimal calculations agree. Input rounding cannot reverse these signs. These are capacity-equivalent counts, not cargo value, mass or final consumption.

The three selected leads were composition, broad modal concordance, and a rate/quantity bottleneck. Composition was feasible and is supported descriptively. National-demand and diversion explanations lack neighboring-port, cargo-mix and operating controls. BTS seasonal revisions and independently measured rail quantities were unavailable, so modal concordance is blocked. USDA corridor rates, throughput, fuel basis and operational disruptions were unavailable, so the bottleneck test is blocked. A fetched title is not a measured comparison.

## Limitations

Current-vintage values do not establish what was known in August 2025. Same-month comparison controls only coarse seasonality; calendar, terminal and routing changes remain plausible. No causal identification, calibrated probability or investment implication follows. The interrupted first collection pass was not replayed; one returned source still has an unresolved durable request record. Its discovery text supplies no numerical test.
