## Interpretation and transmission

**OBSERVATION:** selected September well-depth comparisons are mostly greater than their stated baselines, with one improving sign. The result is local, selected and older than the requested week.

**INFERENCE:** retained observations are consistent with lower groundwater at some sampled wells. Rainfall timing, pumping, aquifer dynamics, measurement timing and rounding remain plausible alternatives.

**ASSUMPTION:** greater displayed depth represents lower groundwater at each sampled well under a stable datum. That assumption does not supply crop exposure or water volume.

**SPECULATION:** persistent water stress could affect agriculture where crops depend on the sampled aquifer during a vulnerable stage. That exposure is unmeasured. No direction or magnitude is assigned to national food supply, consumer prices, company earnings, securities or market forecasts.

A credible transmission test would connect exact geography and crop acreage, irrigation dependence, growth stage, compatible weather/hydrology exposure, realized or revised output and matched controls. A grain-balance test would then separate production from use, imports, exports and beginning stocks. Neither chain is complete.
