## Hypotheses and tests

The initial exploratory plan registered three competing explanations before new source access: **H1**, a dated hard-power event occurred in the selected scope; **H2**, recycled reporting, mismatched dates or changed coverage creates apparent escalation; **H3**, a supported event has measurable economic transmission. Event and publication dates, original attribution, action versus threat/exercise, common notifications and contrary evidence were required controls. The conversation had already disclosed earlier access gaps, so the investigation is not blinded to those limitations.

**T1 — Original-document chronology and attribution: blocked.** Neither newly attempted publisher supplied a document. An offline eligibility audit was run on the actual empty document set and rejected it because a reviewed original document is required. That rejection is an input-quality check, not an executed event-verification test. No synthetic fixture, search snippet, landing page or failure message was promoted into an original event record. H1 and H2 remain unresolved.

**T2 — Contrary evidence and corroboration: blocked.** No current original case record was available to compare with an independent contrary account. The failed alternative read does not show that contrary evidence does not exist. An official participant statement, if later obtained, would remain an attributed claim unless adequate independent original evidence supported the occurrence. Different publishers repeating one military statement or member-state notification would not supply independent confirmation.

**T3 — Economic transmission: not tested.** No compatible event/exposure pair, cost or quantity outcome, pre-event trend, unaffected comparison region or lag specification could be applied to actual observations. Alternative explanations include general demand, weather, ordinary congestion, fuel costs, contract composition, rerouting, inventory buffers and concurrent policy changes. No economic effect follows from a military announcement or an incident count alone.

**D1 — Historical retained-count audit: completed, limited to accounting.** An explicitly exploratory amendment registered this calculation after the retained values had been inspected. The IMO selection reports **61** cumulative confirmed notified incidents since January 10, 2024 to an unknown page-as-of date, including **57** through December 31, 2025. That residual is **not assigned to 2026 or the present week**: the cumulative endpoint, revision history and complete comparability are not established. A separate retained **17**-incident count covers November 2023 through January 9, 2024 under an earlier reporting regime. It was not added to the later total or used as a rate baseline. No transit denominator or equal-duration comparison exists. Therefore no annual growth rate, attack probability, current escalation trend, cargo-loss quantity or shipping-cost effect was calculated.

The computational cross-check uses the same three source cells. It is not independent publisher validation. This historical context cannot resolve any of the current event hypotheses.
