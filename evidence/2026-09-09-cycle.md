## E-2026-09-09-03 — Treasury enlarges long-duration buyback; yields rise after announcement

**Available:** 2026-09-09  
**Source:** Reuters / U.S. Treasury announcement  
**URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/us-treasury-buy-up-6-billion-sept-10-buyback-operation-2026-09-09/

**OBSERVATION:** Treasury said it would buy up to **$6 billion** of 10- to 20-year bonds in its Sep. 10 liquidity-support buyback, triple the size of its prior long-dated operation. Benchmark 10-year yields rose after the announcement and reached **4.8528%** intraday; Reuters reported some investors had expected a larger operation.

**INFERENCE:** The direction of the announcement response reinforces the existing causal-graph distinction between announcement/expectations effects and realized purchase-flow effects. An announced buyback is not mechanically yield-lowering; its market effect depends on the surprise relative to expectations and competing inflation, supply, growth and term-premium forces. No realized-flow credit is assigned before execution.
