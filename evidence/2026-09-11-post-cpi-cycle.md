## E002 — Preliminary September consumer sentiment and inflation expectations

**OBSERVATION:** The University of Michigan preliminary September Consumer Sentiment Index fell to **47.8** from **51.7** in August; Current Economic Conditions were **50.9** and Expectations **45.8**. Reuters reported one-year inflation expectations at **4.6%**, up from 4.0%, and five-year expectations at **3.4%**, up from 3.3%.

**Available:** 2026-09-11 10:00 ET.  
**Sources:** University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers; Reuters.

**INFERENCE:** Higher household inflation expectations support H003's expectations/policy-constraint channel, while the sharp sentiment decline and weak expectations index preserve H002/H001 demand-side disinflation alternatives. Because gasoline and geopolitical stress can move both sentiment and inflation expectations together, this is not independent evidence for a persistent core-inflation mechanism.
