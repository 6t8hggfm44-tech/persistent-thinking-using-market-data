## E2026-08-14-01 — July retail sales materially undershoot consensus

**Available:** 2026-08-14 08:30 ET  
**Source:** Reuters reporting on U.S. Census Bureau release  
**URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/us-retail-sales-unexpectedly-fall-july-2026-08-14/

**OBSERVATION:** Nominal retail sales fell 0.6% m/m in July after an unrevised +0.2% in June, versus Reuters-consensus +0.1%; sales were still +5.0% y/y. The GDP-relevant control group fell 0.4% versus consensus +0.3%. Prime Day timing, lower gasoline receipts, and fading tax-refund support were identified as partial distortions. Food-services/drinking-place sales rose 0.5%.

**INFERENCE:** The control-group decline makes the weakness harder to dismiss as gasoline alone and increases the probability that real consumer-demand growth is slowing. It is meaningful evidence against the strongest version of H001 and for H002, but one month with unusual event timing is insufficient to establish recession. Nominal retail sales are also an incomplete measure of total consumer services spending.

**Expectedness:** H001 — materially surprising if repeated; one print is a warning, not a rejection. H002 — supportive. H003 — ambiguous because weaker demand is disinflationary while energy/geopolitical inflation remains active. H004 — mildly adverse if demand weakness broadens into earnings/output, but not a direct productivity test.
