## E1 — July core PCE monthly inflation is contained at 0.2%, while annual inflation remains elevated

**Available:** 2026-08-26 08:30 ET  
**Source:** U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-july-2026 ; Reuters cross-check, https://www.reuters.com/business/us-inflation-remains-sticky-july-2nd-quarter-gdp-unrevised-15-2026-08-26/

**OBSERVATION:** The first July Personal Income and Outlays release reported the PCE price index up **0.2% m/m and 3.7% y/y**. Core PCE excluding food and energy rose **0.2% m/m and 3.3% y/y**. Personal income rose **0.4%**, disposable personal income **0.5%**, nominal PCE **0.2%**, real DPI **0.4%**, and real PCE was essentially flat at less than 0.1% growth (0.0% at published one-decimal precision). Reuters reported the headline annual rate was above its 3.6% consensus and noted that the unrounded core monthly rise was approximately 0.246%, close to the rounding boundary for 0.3%.

**INFERENCE:** The 0.2% core monthly result is direct contrary evidence to an immediate core-inflation reacceleration and correctly resolves P000021's >=0.3% event false. At the same time, 3.7% headline and 3.3% core annual inflation remain well above the Fed's target, so one contained monthly core print does not eliminate H003's broader inflation/policy-constraint regime. The combination of stronger real income and flat real spending also reinforces the existing household-bifurcation model rather than proving either broad strength or recession.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: supportive on the monthly core-inflation margin; mixed because annual inflation remains high and real spending was flat.
- H002: mixed; flat real spending helps the household-weakness branch, while real income growth reduces immediate household stress.
- H003: adverse to an immediate core-acceleration version; compatible with the broader sticky-above-target/policy-constraint version.
- H004: little direct productivity information.

## E2 — Broader demand and investment data are stronger than a simple recession story

**Available:** 2026-08-26 morning  
**Source:** Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/business/us-inflation-remains-sticky-july-2nd-quarter-gdp-unrevised-15-2026-08-26/

**OBSERVATION:** Reuters reported July durable-goods orders rose **1.1%**, with nondefense capital goods excluding aircraft up **0.2%** and related shipments indicating continued business investment. The second estimate of Q2 real GDP remained **1.5% annualized**, but consumer spending was revised up to **3.4%** and final sales to private domestic purchasers to **4.2%**. Corporate profits increased by roughly **$400.9 billion** in Q2, the second-largest increase on record according to Reuters.

**INFERENCE:** This weakens H002's current-generalized-contraction branch and reinforces the model's composition-sensitive expansion: household/housing channels can be soft while business investment and other private demand remain strong. It also raises the importance of the H001-versus-H003 distinction: strong demand is benign only if price/rate pressure continues to ease.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: supportive through resilient private demand and investment.
- H002: contrary to a current broad contraction, though not to a later rate-sensitive downturn.
- H003: also compatible because strong demand alongside above-target inflation can keep the policy constraint binding.
- H004: investment spending is a prerequisite for a productivity boom but is not itself proof of realized productivity.

## E3 — Nvidia confirms extraordinary AI compute demand, not economy-wide productivity

**Available:** 2026-08-26 after U.S. close and before cutoff  
**Source:** NVIDIA earnings release, https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027

**OBSERVATION:** Nvidia reported fiscal Q2 revenue of **$96.2 billion**, up **106% y/y**, and Data Center revenue of **$89.0 billion**, up **117% y/y**. The company also guided to continued strong revenue in the next quarter.

**INFERENCE:** This materially confirms the existence of very strong AI-compute investment demand and supports the already-recorded AI/capex-demand node. It does **not** directly establish H004's economy-wide productivity-boom thesis. Vendor revenue, data-center spending, and capex are inputs/investment proxies; H004 requires broader evidence that output per unit of labor/capital, real output, margins, or real incomes are improving because of that investment. Treating demand for AI infrastructure as realized aggregate productivity would be a proxy-scope error.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: compatible through business-investment resilience.
- H002: contrary to broad current capex retrenchment, though concentrated AI spending can coexist with household weakness.
- H003: compatible through financing/duration demand and strong nominal investment.
- H004: directionally encouraging but insufficient for a weight increase absent direct productivity/output-efficiency evidence.

## E4 — Oil falls further and the market reaction to inflation remains modest rather than disorderly

**Available:** 2026-08-26 after U.S. settlements/close  
**Source:** Reuters global markets wrap, https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-wrapup-1-2026-08-26/

**OBSERVATION:** WTI settled at **$82.23** and Brent at **$87.84**, extending the retreat from the prior week's highs while Iran/Oman talks continued and physical uncertainty around the Strait of Hormuz remained. Reuters reported the S&P 500 closed essentially flat at **7,675.70**, while the U.S. 10-year yield was around **4.65%** after the inflation data. Immediate post-release rate-hike pricing rose, but the market response remained modest rather than a disorderly inflation repricing.

**INFERENCE:** Falling oil is additional contrary evidence to H003's immediate energy-escalation branch and reduces near-term pass-through pressure at the margin. The contained cross-asset response also argues against treating one above-consensus headline inflation reading as a regime break. However, the persistence of above-target annual inflation and stronger private demand leaves the policy-constraint branch live.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: modestly supportive through lower energy pressure and stable financial conditions.
- H002: little direct support; the oil decline appears substantially geopolitical/supply-expectation driven rather than a clean demand-collapse signal.
- H003: adverse to the energy-escalation sub-branch; mixed for the broader regime because inflation remains above target and strong demand persists.
- H004: little direct information.

## Combined inference

Today's evidence narrows rather than resolves the H001/H003 contest. The monthly core PCE print and falling oil argue against an immediate inflation acceleration, but annual inflation remains high while investment and revised private demand are stronger than a broad recession story. The result is a small weight transfer from H002 to H003, not because P000021 resolved favorably, but because the separately observed post-cutoff combination is **stronger real-side demand plus still-binding inflation/policy risk**. H001 remains close because monthly core inflation and energy pressure are easing. Nvidia's results strengthen the AI-investment-demand node but are explicitly not treated as direct H004 productivity evidence.
