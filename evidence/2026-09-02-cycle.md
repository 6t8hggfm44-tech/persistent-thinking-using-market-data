## E1 — ADP confirms weak private hiring, but is not the BLS payroll target
**Available:** 2026-09-02 08:15 ET  
**Sources:** ADP National Employment Report; Reuters contemporaneous report  
**ADP:** https://adpemploymentreport.com/  
**Reuters:** https://www.reuters.com/business/us-private-payrolls-growth-slows-august-adp-says-2026-09-02/

**OBSERVATION:** ADP reported **+38,000** private-sector jobs in August, below the roughly 48,000 economist expectation cited by Reuters. Education/health, leisure/hospitality, construction, and financial activities added jobs, while manufacturing and professional/business services lost jobs.

**INFERENCE:** This strengthens the existing low-hire side of the labor-flow model and modestly raises concern about broad labor demand. It does **not** resolve or validate P000025: ADP private payrolls and BLS total nonfarm payrolls are distinct measurements with different coverage/methods. Treating the numerical match between ADP's +38K and P000025's frozen +38K BLS point estimate as forecast success would be proxy-scope substitution and hindsight assimilation. The more discriminating remaining question is whether weak hiring is accompanied by higher separations/claims and a higher unemployment rate.

**Available:** 2026-09-02 14:00 ET  
**Source:** Federal Reserve Beige Book summary  
**URL:** https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beigebook202608-summary.htm

**OBSERVATION:** The Fed reported that economic activity **edged up**, employment increased only **slightly**, and prices increased **moderately** across most Districts. Healthy labor demand appeared most often in manufacturing, construction, and selected services, while retail and hospitality saw weaker labor demand. **INFERENCE:** This is consistent with a low-hire/low-fire, compositionally uneven economy rather than synchronized contraction. It also keeps H003's input-cost/policy-constraint branch alive, but the report does not show broad accelerating final-demand inflation. Its collection cutoff predates much of the latest oil escalation, so it is a lagged cross-check rather than a current high-frequency read.

## E3 — July factory-orders rebound is headline-strong but composition-limited
**Available:** 2026-09-02 10:00 ET  
**Source:** U.S. Census factory-orders release as contemporaneously reported by Reuters  
**Reuters:** https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/rise-us-factory-orders-beats-expectations-july-2026-09-02/

**OBSERVATION:** July factory orders rose **0.9% m/m**, above the roughly 0.6% expectation, after a revised 0.2% June decline. The gain was heavily driven by a **12.7%** increase in civilian aircraft and parts. Nondefense capital-goods orders excluding aircraft were **flat** in July, while shipments of those core capital goods rose **1.2%**.

**INFERENCE:** The release argues against a broad manufacturing collapse, but the headline is not a clean aggregate-demand signal because aircraft composition explains much of the rebound. It reinforces the existing composition-sensitive activity model and provides little reason to raise H004: investment demand is not equivalent to realized productivity.

## Skeptic against pre-update leader H003
The strongest contrary case is that the latest direct labor evidence is weak rather than inflationary: ADP private hiring slowed to +38K and the Beige Book described only slight employment gains. Factory orders look stronger in the headline than in the core composition, and the Beige Book's price language is moderate rather than a broad acceleration. The oil shock can produce stagflationary pressure and weaker real demand rather than the resilient nominal-growth state H003 would prefer.

Hidden assumptions to reject: (1) that ADP maps one-for-one into BLS payrolls; (2) that higher oil/long yields independently identify persistent core inflation; (3) that a headline factory-orders beat indicates broad demand; and (4) that Beige Book observations through Aug. 24 fully capture the late-August/early-September energy escalation.

The clean near-term discriminator is **separation flow**: if 2026-09-03 initial claims rise materially above the recent low-200K range, weak hiring is beginning to propagate into layoffs and H002 becomes more plausible. If claims remain near ~200K despite weak ADP hiring, the low-hire/low-fire decomposition survives and synchronized recession transmission remains unconfirmed.
