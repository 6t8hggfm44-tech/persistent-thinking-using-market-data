## Phase 3 — Skeptic against H001

H001 entered as the narrow leader at 0.35. The strongest serious countercase is that today's positive aggregate industrial-production print is unusually composition-dependent: consumer-goods output fell while AI-linked equipment and defense output were strong, and housing deteriorated sharply. A narrow investment/manufacturing boom can coexist with weakening household demand. At the same time, oil remains above $84 WTI and long yields remain near multi-year highs, preserving a restrictive financial-conditions channel that can worsen housing and eventually labor/consumption. The S&P/tech decline is consistent with that risk but is not causal proof.

Counterevidence to the attack is that total industrial production and manufacturing both rose 0.2%, directly falsifying P000015's <=0.0% aggregate event; the latest verified HY OAS remained tight at the prior cycle; and the industrial details show genuine strength in business equipment rather than a purely statistical utility swing. H002 therefore should not gain from the housing data without acknowledging that its expected broader real-activity contraction failed this specific prospective test.

### Structural refinement

Add a **composition-sensitive activity** refinement: aggregate industrial production can mask opposing sector channels. AI/capex/defense demand can lift business-equipment and high-tech output while household-sensitive consumer goods, autos, and housing weaken. Future H001/H002/H004 discrimination should therefore use aggregate production jointly with composition, labor, credit, and household-demand measures rather than treating the sign of total IP as sufficient.
