**Source:** Federal Reserve Bank of New York Survey of Consumer Expectations, contemporaneously reported by Reuters  
**URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/consumers-more-worried-about-personal-finances-jobs-new-york-fed-report-shows-2026-09-08/

**OBSERVATION:** August survey results showed one-year inflation expectations steady at **3.6%**, five-year expectations steady at **3.0%**, and three-year expectations edging down to **3.2%**. Expectations of a higher unemployment rate rose sharply, confidence in finding new work weakened, and views of household finances/credit access deteriorated.

**INFERENCE:** This is meaningful counterevidence to a simple H003 story in which the current energy shock is already unanchoring medium-term expectations. At the same time, the labor/financial-expectation deterioration preserves an H002 pathway. Survey expectations are not realized inflation or realized labor outcomes, so neither inference is promoted into a hard-state change.

**Expected under hypotheses:**
- **H001:** stable medium-term inflation expectations supportive; worsening labor/financial sentiment adverse.
- **H002:** labor/financial deterioration supportive, but still soft survey evidence rather than hard contraction data.
- **H003:** stable-to-lower medium-term inflation expectations are contrary evidence; one-year expectations remain elevated.
- **H004:** largely non-discriminating.
