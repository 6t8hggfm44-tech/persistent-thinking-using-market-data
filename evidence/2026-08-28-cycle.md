## Skeptic against pre-update leader H003
The strongest countercase is H001, not H002. Oil has fallen sharply from last week's peak, medium-term inflation expectations are still described by the Fed Chair as anchored, and the chair explicitly avoided committing to a particular decision. A credible path remains: resilient labor/private demand persists, lower energy and eventual policy credibility pull inflation down, and high yields ease without recession. The BLS benchmark revision and weak sentiment also show that nominal resilience is not uniform.

The largest hidden assumption in an H003 upgrade would be to attribute the 6 bp one-day 10-year rise to Warsh's speech or “persistent inflation” alone. Positioning, term premium and other Treasury-specific factors can generate the same endpoint. P000022 itself assigned the >=4.70% event only 47% and lost to the neutral binary benchmark, so the system must not rewrite that uncertainty after the fact.

The strongest prospective discriminator between H003 and H001 is no longer Friday's yield move. It is whether **August inflation and employment data show persistent underlying price pressure without a material labor deterioration**.
