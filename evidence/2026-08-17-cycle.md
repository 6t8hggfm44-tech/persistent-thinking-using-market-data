## Phase 3 — Skeptic against H001

H001 entered as the narrow leader at 0.34. The strongest serious countercase is that today's combination of rising oil, a 10-year yield near 4.72%, a 30-year yield at a multi-decade high, and negative broad equity-sector breadth is exactly the kind of supply/term-premium pressure that can prevent a benign soft landing even if contemporaneous price data look better. Tight HY spreads are a lagging market cross-check and can remain complacent early in a downturn. The Empire State survey is regional and volatile, and its rise in prices paid is not purely benign. The earlier national labor and retail weakness therefore remains unresolved rather than falsified.

Counterevidence to that attack is substantial: the newly available HY OAS tightened to 2.67% rather than widening, and the Empire State activity index surprised strongly to the upside. Those observations make an already-broadening H002 recession mechanism less likely today. The result is not a clean H001 confirmation because the H003 oil/long-rate channel simultaneously strengthened.
