## Skeptic against pre-update leader H003
The strongest countercase is that Monday's most dramatic evidence is an **event shock**, not yet a demonstration of persistent underlying inflation. Oil moved higher on renewed military conflict after falling sharply the prior week; one reversal does not establish a durable trend. The Chicago/Dallas split shows that real-activity signals remain bifurcated, and the modest equity decline is not acute financial stress. Market-implied Fed probabilities and long yields are jointly endogenous downstream endpoints and must not be counted as independent confirmations of the same inflation story.

The most important way H003 can fail is still prospective: if the September 4 employment report shows broad labor deterioration, or if September 11 core CPI is benign despite resilient activity, the current policy-constraint thesis should lose weight. Conversely, persistent energy pressure plus resilient employment and sticky core CPI would discriminate more strongly in H003's favor than today's price moves alone.
