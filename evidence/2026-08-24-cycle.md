## E1 — July Chicago Fed National Activity Index

**Available:** 2026-08-24 08:30 ET  
**Source:** Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago / FRED, https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CFNAI ; component summary cross-check: https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/chicago-fed-national-activity-index/news/577617

**OBSERVATION:** July CFNAI was **-0.08**, versus a revised **+0.06** in June; zero represents historical-trend growth and a negative reading represents below-average growth. The three-month average fell to **-0.04** from +0.01. Production contributed +0.01, sales/orders/inventories +0.02, employment -0.01 (improved from -0.05), and personal consumption/housing **-0.09** (down from +0.04).

**INFERENCE:** The July national data reinforce the existing composition-sensitive model: weakness was concentrated heavily in household/consumption/housing while employment contribution improved. This is modest support for H002's delayed household-downturn branch, but it does not establish generalized contraction and is partly superseded temporally by the strong August flash services/composite evidence already in state.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: mixed/slightly adverse; below-trend July activity is adverse, but improving employment contribution and later strong August services remain compatible with stabilization.
- H002: supportive, especially the household/housing component, but weaker than a broad decline across employment/production.
- H003: mixed; softer household activity can reduce inflation/rate pressure, while it does not directly test the fiscal/duration branch.
- H004: little direct information about economy-wide productivity.

## E2 — Treasury clarification on enlarged long-duration buybacks

**Available:** 2026-08-24, Reuters report before the cutoff  
**Source:** Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-treasury-stick-debt-auction-schedule-despite-bigger-buybacks-bessent-says-2026-08-24/

**OBSERVATION:** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Treasury will keep the regular auction schedule despite larger long-duration buybacks. Reuters reports that the enlarged purchases **have not yet occurred** and are scheduled to begin **September 10** for longer-dated securities. The funding source was not specified; use of the Treasury General Account was discussed as a possibility.

**INFERENCE / CORRECTION:** The Aug. 19 long-yield rally cannot be evidence of realized enlarged-buyback purchase flow because that flow had not occurred. Any pre-Sept. 10 effect belongs to an **announcement / expectations / liquidity-signaling** channel. The prospective purchase-flow / duration-absorption channel remains a separate hypothesis to be tested only after purchases actually begin. Regular auction supply continuing also limits any inference that buybacks replace structural issuance pressure.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: modestly compatible with an expectations/liquidity channel easing long rates without weaker growth.
- H002: ambiguous; lower yields can also arise from weaker demand.
- H003: mixed. The clarification weakens attribution of recent yield relief to realized buyback flow, while the unchanged auction schedule preserves structural duration-supply pressure.
- H004: little direct information.

**Source:** Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/oil-falls-1-ahead-us-announcement-impose-further-sanctions-iran-2026-08-23/

**OBSERVATION:** Brent settled at **$92.17** and WTI at **$85.01**, each down **2.35%**, despite expanded U.S. sanctions related to Iran. Reuters reported that much of the announcement was anticipated and that the sanctions' effect depends materially on responses by major importers such as China. **INFERENCE:** This is contrary evidence to the most immediate version of H003's energy-escalation branch: additional sanctions did not produce another price jump. It is only a modest downgrade because oil remains elevated and physical/geopolitical constraints remain active.

**Expectedness:**
- H001: modestly supportive through lower immediate energy/inflation pressure.
- H002: mixed; lower oil eases household pressure but can also reflect demand expectations.
- H003: mildly adverse in the short run; not a falsification of later pass-through because price levels remain high.
- H004: little direct information.

## Combined inference

The post-cutoff evidence modestly narrows H003's lead rather than reversing the regime call. July CFNAI supplies fresh national evidence of household/housing weakness, while Monday oil/yield relief is mild contrary evidence to an immediate inflation-duration squeeze.
