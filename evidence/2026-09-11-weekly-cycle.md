## E001 — August Producer Price Index

**Available:** 2026-09-10 08:30 ET  
**Source:** U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, *Producer Price Indexes — August 2026*  
**URL:** https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm

**OBSERVATION:** Final-demand PPI rose **0.4% m/m** in August and **5.4% y/y**. Final-demand goods rose **1.1% m/m**, final-demand services rose **0.1%**, and final demand less foods, energy, and trade services rose **0.3%**. More than three-fourths of the goods increase was attributable to a **4.2%** rise in final-demand energy.

**Forecast link:** P000028's >=0.4% event resolved TRUE exactly at the threshold. The 0.4% point matched the frozen external consensus.

**INFERENCE:** The release keeps the producer-price pipeline firm enough that H003 cannot be dismissed, but the headline's energy contribution and the exactly-consensus result make it weak evidence for broad underlying-inflation persistence. P000023 core CPI remains the more discriminating H001-vs-H003 test.

## E002 — Weekly unemployment claims

**Available:** 2026-09-10 morning ET  
**Source:** U.S. Department of Labor release as contemporaneously reported by Reuters  
**URL:** https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-weekly-jobless-claims-edge-down-layoffs-remain-low-2026-09-10/

**OBSERVATION:** Initial claims for the week ended September 5 edged down to **206,000**. Continuing claims for the week ended August 29 edged down to **1.774 million**.

**INFERENCE:** This is additional evidence for the low-separation/low-layoff leg of the v0.2.14 labor decomposition and against an immediate generalized labor contraction. It does **not** validate a monthly CES payroll-magnitude bridge; P000025 already showed why those measurement layers must remain separate.

## E003 — Existing-home sales

**Available:** 2026-09-10  
**Source:** National Association of Realtors release as contemporaneously reported by Reuters  
**URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/us-existing-home-sales-drop-14-month-low-august-2026-09-10/

**OBSERVATION:** August existing-home sales fell **2.0%** to a **3.98 million** seasonally adjusted annual rate, the lowest in 14 months. Inventory rose to **1.62 million** units, or **4.9 months** of supply.

**INFERENCE:** Household/housing weakness remains a meaningful delayed-downside channel even while layoffs are low and payrolls have recently strengthened. This prevents the labor-resilience evidence from being promoted into a uniformly strong-demand narrative.

## E004 — Treasury long-end buyback and yields

**Available:** 2026-09-10  
**Sources:** U.S. Treasury program materials; Reuters market coverage  
**URLs:** https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0607 ; https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/edgy-bond-investors-unconsoled-by-bessents-big-buyback-2026-09-10/

**OBSERVATION:** Treasury's enlarged long-end liquidity-support buyback program was active, with a maximum operation size of **$6 billion** for the September 10 operation. Long Treasury yields nevertheless rose on the day; Reuters reported 10-, 20-, and 30-year yields climbing and emphasized the operation's small scale relative to the Treasury market.

**INFERENCE:** The realized-flow channel is now observable rather than merely announced, but one day's yield response does not identify the causal effect of the buyback because PPI, oil, policy expectations, deficits, auctions, and global duration supply were simultaneous drivers. The observation is consistent with the existing model's claim that liquidity-support buybacks are tactical and need not dominate structural duration/inflation pressure; it does not justify a new causal edge.

**URL:** https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/brent-holds-above-100-tanker-attacks-deepen-supply-fear-2026-09-10/

**OBSERVATION:** Brent crude settled **6% higher at $107.63/bbl** and WTI at **$102.48/bbl**, both above $100, amid further tanker attacks and disruption to Middle East oil flows.

**INFERENCE:** The joint direction of oil, yields, and policy pricing is more compatible with H003's current policy-constraint state than with a fully benign H001 state, but market prices are downstream beliefs rather than direct evidence of the Fed decision or persistent core inflation. P000027 remains frozen at 58% and is not rewritten to follow the market.

## Evidence synthesis

The post-cutoff evidence is compositionally split: low layoffs and firm producer prices coexist with weak housing; the September oil shock and higher yields tighten the forward constraint but do not reveal August core CPI. The strongest new H003 evidence is the **joint** energy/rates/policy-pricing state, not P000028's threshold success by itself. The strongest contrary evidence is continued housing weakness plus the fact that August PPI matched consensus and was materially energy-driven.
