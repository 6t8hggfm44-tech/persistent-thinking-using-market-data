## E001 — August flash PMI: services accelerates, manufacturing slows

**Available:** 2026-08-21 09:45 ET first flash release; Reuters report published 2026-08-21.  
**Source:** S&P Global flash PMI via Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/business/us-service-sector-fuels-acceleration-business-activity-sp-global-says-2026-08-21/

**OBSERVATION:** August flash services PMI rose to **56.8** from 54.6, the strongest since December 2024; composite output rose to **56.0** from 54.5, the strongest since April 2022. Manufacturing PMI eased to **53.2** from 53.9. S&P Global said services hiring increased at the fastest pace in 19 months. Manufacturing output/order momentum was restrained by reduced safety-stock building and Iran-war supply disruption. Input and selling-price growth eased but remained elevated.

**INFERENCE:** Broad near-term activity is stronger than H002's generalized-contraction path predicts, but the composition is not the broad manufacturing acceleration implied by the strong Empire State and Philadelphia headline surveys. Strong services plus still-elevated prices is compatible with both H001 and H003; it is not uniquely benign soft-landing evidence.

**Hypothesis impact:** H001 positive on real activity; H002 materially negative; H003 mixed-to-positive because strong demand can sustain policy/inflation pressure; H004 little direct information because the release does not measure productivity.

## E002 — AI-related corporate duration supply shows investor absorption costs

**Available:** Reuters report published 2026-08-21.  
**Source:** https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/us-corporate-ai-debt-surge-tests-investor-limits-fatigue-emerges-2026-08-21/

**OBSERVATION:** BNP Paribas data cited by Reuters put 2026 AI-hyperscaler debt issuance at **$220 billion as of Aug. 10**, versus **$12.5 billion** in the comparable prior-year period. Reuters reported technology investment-grade spreads around **89 bp**, about 9 bp wider than the overall investment-grade market, and larger new-issue concessions as investors absorb repeated long-duration offerings.

**INFERENCE:** This is new external support for the model's Aug. 17 provisional private-duration-supply pathway. It supports the existence and scale of the supply channel, but does **not** identify how many basis points of the Treasury-yield move it caused; fiscal supply, inflation risk, growth, and Fed expectations remain competing causes.

**Hypothesis impact:** H003 positive; H004 neutral-to-mixed because financing intensity documents AI investment scale but not realized productivity.

## E003 — Treasury buyback relief proves short-lived in market yields

**Available:** 2026-08-21 market session.  
**Source:** Reuters global markets report: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-08-21/

**OBSERVATION:** The U.S. 10-year Treasury yield was about **4.73%** and the 30-year about **5.266%** on Aug. 21, both higher on the day. Reuters reported that the earlier rally after Treasury's expanded long-end buyback announcement had largely reversed; the 2-year also rose after the stronger PMI data.

**INFERENCE:** Treasury debt-management can affect liquidity and yields tactically, but the Aug. 19 move should not be modeled as a durable structural offset to growth/inflation/fiscal/private-duration pressure. The rebound is not proof that one of those rival forces caused the move.

**Hypothesis impact:** H003 positive relative to H001; H002 negative-to-neutral because strong activity rather than recession fear accompanied part of the yield rise.
