## Reconciliation and independence

Freeze independent domain reports before synthesis. Match real source/event periods, exposure, units, geography, denominator populations and plausible lags. Preserve OBSERVATION, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION and SPECULATION. State prior exposure to other reports or outcomes. National monthly data, company filings, weekly stocks and aircraft tracks are not interchangeable measurements.

Audit upstream source/release IDs and common causes. Twelve documents citing one release are one underlying evidence item. BLS inputs reused by labor, housing and corporate reports, Census inputs reused across manufacturing/consumer/fiscal, port figures reused by freight/trade, and petroleum data reused by both energy producers require explicit source-dependence treatment. Different report authors do not establish independent measurement. Unknown ancestry stays unknown.

Require executed tests and operational/economic evidence for causal transmission. Retain contradictions, insufficient overlap, missing controls and untested links. Weather remains independently authored before physical-to-economic reconciliation; demand adjustments require executed controls. GEV movement counts do not measure cargo volume or establish intent.
