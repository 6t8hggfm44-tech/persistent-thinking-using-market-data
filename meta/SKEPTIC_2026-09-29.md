# Skeptic — 2026-09-29

**Target:** H003 — fiscal / inflation regime, prior weight 0.40.

The strongest contrary case is that several September 29 observations fit a cooling-demand / high-rate transition rather than durable inflation persistence. August job openings fell to 7.079 million, Consumer Confidence and Expectations fell sharply, and Brent declined as Middle East export flows improved. These developments weaken the assumption that the energy shock and nominal-demand impulse will continue intensifying in a straight line.

Long yields near multi-decade highs are not a clean H003 identifier. Term premium, duration supply, investment demand, positioning and policy uncertainty can raise long yields even if realized inflation later cools. Consumer pessimism may also reflect high prices and geopolitical stress rather than predict actual consumption one-for-one.

The contrary case is capped by low layoffs, little-changed hires, nominal home-price resilience, contained recent credit evidence and Brent still above $100. Those observations do not support an immediate generalized recession either.

**Discriminators:** first-release August core PCE on September 30, followed by first-release September payrolls on October 2. A soft core-PCE print plus weak payrolls would materially weaken H003; sticky core PCE with resilient payrolls would favor H003 over H002.
