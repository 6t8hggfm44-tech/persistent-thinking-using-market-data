# Skeptic Report — 2026-08-08

The leading hypothesis is H002 (late-cycle recession) at only 0.32, so it is not a conviction call.

## Strongest case against H002

- Equity indexes are at record highs rather than displaying broad risk aversion.
- Small caps have also participated strongly, inconsistent with a purely narrow defensive rally.
- Credit spreads, on the latest verified observation available to this run, remained historically tight.
- A single payroll contraction can be distorted by seasonal adjustment and subsequent revision.
- Falling oil can relieve inflation pressure and improve real household purchasing power.

## Hidden assumptions in H002

1. Payroll weakness will persist.
2. Earnings will respond negatively rather than being protected by productivity/margin gains.
3. Credit markets will eventually validate the labor signal.
4. Inflation will not force the Fed to tighten into the slowdown.

## Highest-information discriminator

If labor remains weak over the next 1–3 months **and** high-yield spreads widen materially while equity breadth deteriorates, H002 gains substantially. If labor weakness persists but spreads remain tight and earnings estimates hold, H001 becomes more likely.
