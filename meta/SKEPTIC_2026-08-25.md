# Skeptic Review — 2026-08-25

**Leading hypotheses before review:** H001 Soft Landing and H003 Fiscal / Inflation Regime, tied at 0.34  
**Tie-break for adversarial focus:** H003 is attacked because fresh oil evidence directly challenges one of its active branches.  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-25T17:23:09-04:00

## Strongest case against H003

1. **The immediate energy-squeeze branch weakened again.** Brent fell 3.9% to $88.58 and WTI 3.1% to $82.36 even though Hormuz traffic remained constrained and geopolitical risk persisted. The market is explicitly assigning less supply risk to the current sanctions/economic-pressure path than to military escalation. A model that simply carries elevated oil forward would now be overfitting the earlier shock.
2. **Fresh household data are weak enough to provide a disinflationary rival mechanism.** New-home sales fell 10.5% to 607,000 and the Conference Board Expectations Index fell to 68.2. If rate-sensitive household demand continues weakening, softer demand can reduce inflation pressure and long yields without validating H003's fiscal/duration story.
3. **The Fed evidence is conditional and narrow.** Collins said tightening may be needed if disinflation stalls; she also retained gradual disinflation as her base case. One policymaker's conditional language should not be promoted into a forecast of actual FOMC tightening.
4. **Consumer inflation expectations are not realized inflation.** The reported rise to 5.8% from 5.6% may partly reflect salient gasoline/food/conflict concerns. It is evidence about expectations, not a substitute for the upcoming PCE release or market-based inflation compensation.

## Hidden assumptions under H003

- The remaining oil/geopolitical premium will transmit into broader inflation rather than unwind further.
- Strong services demand will remain strong enough that household weakness does not broaden into a material growth slowdown.
- Elevated long rates will continue to reflect inflation/duration pressure rather than increasingly weak demand.
- Conditional hawkish rhetoric will translate into policy or market pricing if inflation remains sticky.

## Strongest response

H003 should remain live and narrowly leading, not because every bearish-rate observation can be narrated into it, but because the model still has independent support outside spot oil: inflation expectations moved higher, core inflation remains above target, the Fed reaction function is explicitly conditional on further disinflation, public/private duration pressure remains in the causal graph, and recent services activity was strong. The oil decline therefore removes some immediate energy pressure without eliminating the broader inflation/policy-constraint regime.

H001 also deserves a skeptical constraint: repeated housing weakness and deteriorating forward household expectations make it harder to call the current expansion uniformly benign. The improved jobs differential prevents that evidence from being read as a current generalized contraction.

## Distinguishing observations

- **H001-favoring:** July core PCE at or below 0.2% m/m, followed by softer inflation expectations/long yields while services activity remains resilient.
- **H003-favoring:** July core PCE at or above 0.3% m/m, or continued firm inflation expectations/policy pricing despite resilient growth and lower spot oil.
- **H002-favoring:** household/housing weakness broadens into weaker hiring/re-employment, rising initial claims, credit widening, or broader national activity deterioration.

## Verdict

Do not make a large regime change. Move 0.01 from H001 to H002 because the household/housing weakness received another national confirmation. Keep H003 unchanged at 0.34 because lower oil is offset by firmer inflation expectations and a still-hawkish conditional Fed reaction function. H003 becomes the narrow leader by one point, with H001 a close second. No new structural causal edge is justified from today's evidence.
