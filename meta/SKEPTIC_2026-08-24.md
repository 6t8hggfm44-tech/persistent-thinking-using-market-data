# Skeptic Review — 2026-08-24

**Leading hypothesis before review:** H003 Fiscal / Inflation Regime, weight 0.35  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-24T17:36:26-04:00

## Strongest case against H003

1. **Energy did not respond as the near-term escalation story predicted.** Brent and WTI each fell 2.35% on Aug. 24 despite expanded Iran-related sanctions. If the dominant mechanism were an increasingly binding energy/inflation shock, additional sanctions might have been expected to push prices higher rather than lower.
2. **Treasury buyback attribution was too loose.** Reuters clarified that the enlarged long-duration buybacks have not yet executed and begin Sept. 10. Therefore the Aug. 19 long-yield rally cannot be evidence of realized purchase-flow/duration absorption. At most it is evidence of an announcement/expectations/liquidity-signaling channel.
3. **July national activity was below trend.** CFNAI fell to -0.08 and the personal-consumption/housing component contributed -0.09. A weaker household sector can itself ease inflation and long-rate pressure, meaning lower yields are not uniquely evidence of successful debt-management policy or benign H003-compatible growth.
4. **The model risks narrating every yield move into H003.** Fiscal supply, commodity prices, growth, policy expectations, technical liquidity, private duration, and Treasury communication can all move the long end. H003 becomes unfalsifiable if every move is post-hoc assigned to a different branch.

## Hidden assumptions under H003

- Iran sanctions and reduced energy buffers will materially affect realized oil availability rather than be offset by demand, rerouting, inventories, or sanctions evasion.
- Elevated services activity will persist strongly enough to keep nominal demand and policy pressure high.
- Structural public/private duration supply will continue to matter for long yields after controlling qualitatively for growth and inflation expectations.
- The current level of long yields is economically restrictive enough to transmit into rate-sensitive demand rather than simply reflect stronger nominal growth.

## Strongest response

H003 should be trimmed, not discarded. Brent remains above $90 despite Monday's decline; physical transit constraints and depleted buffers preserve energy upside risk. Treasury's regular auction schedule remains intact, so the clarification weakens the **buyback-flow attribution** but does not remove structural duration supply. August flash services/composite activity remains strong, making a simple demand-collapse explanation incomplete. The most informative H001-vs-H003 tests remain whether core/expected inflation and long yields ease while activity stays resilient.

## Distinguishing observations

- **H001-favoring:** resilient activity plus core inflation at/under expectation, softer inflation expectations, and sustainably lower long yields.
- **H003-favoring:** resilient activity plus upside core-inflation surprises, firmer inflation expectations, or renewed long-yield pressure despite no growth deterioration.
- **H002-favoring:** the July household/housing weakness extends into rising initial claims, persistent continuing claims, broader national activity weakness, credit widening, or earnings deterioration.

## Verdict

Reduce H003 modestly and restore a small amount of H002 weight. Do not upgrade H001 merely because oil and yields fell for one session. The material model correction is to split Treasury **announcement/expectations effects** from **realized purchase-flow effects**, with the latter ineligible for causal credit before execution begins.
