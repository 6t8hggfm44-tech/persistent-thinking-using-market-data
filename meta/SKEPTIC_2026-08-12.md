# Skeptic Review — 2026-08-12

**Target:** H001 Soft Landing, the pre-update numerical leader at 0.31.  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-12T17:54:19-04:00

## Strongest case against increasing H001

1. **The CPI result was not a positive surprise.** July headline CPI at 3.4% YoY and the monthly headline/core readings were essentially in line with published consensus. P000012's favorable proper score cannot be treated as informational evidence for H001 because its event threshold was itself set at the consensus point and the outcome exactly matched consensus.
2. **The inflation problem is moderated, not eliminated.** Headline CPI remains 3.4% YoY and energy remains materially above a year earlier. The latest geopolitical oil shock occurred too late to be fully tested by July CPI, so H003 retains a plausible lagged-pass-through explanation.
3. **Labor weakness is unresolved.** Earlier July employment weakness remains a meaningful H002 input. A benign CPI print can occur during demand deterioration, so disinflation alone does not distinguish soft landing from recession.
4. **Credit is reassuring but lagged.** The Aug. 11 HY OAS reading of 2.72% is still tight and strongly fails to confirm current recession stress, but credit spreads can lag early labor deterioration. One fresh observation should reduce H002, not eliminate it.
5. **Equity resilience is not an independent macro proof.** Aug. 12 index gains were partly driven by strong AI-related earnings. Treating CPI, lower yields, and equity gains as three independent confirmations would overcount a common event plus idiosyncratic earnings news.

## Strongest rival explanations

### H003 Fiscal / Inflation Regime
A contained July CPI can coexist with H003 if long yields remain structurally elevated and the newest oil shock passes through with a lag. H003 would regain weight if PPI or subsequent CPI data accelerate and long yields rise materially in response.

### H002 Late-Cycle Recession
Disinflation may partly reflect weaker demand rather than a healthy soft landing. H002 needs repeated labor weakness plus widening credit spreads and/or broad earnings deterioration before it deserves renewed weight.

## Hidden assumptions under H001

- That current tight credit spreads will remain informative rather than lagging.
- That recent labor weakness is noise/softening rather than the start of contraction.
- That the latest energy shock will not materially reaccelerate broad inflation.
- That strong earnings remain sufficiently broad rather than concentrated in AI/technology beneficiaries.

## Best discriminators from here

- July PPI on Aug. 13 and the associated Treasury-yield reaction.
- Continued daily HY OAS readings after the labor/CPI sequence.
- Retail-sales and earnings-breadth evidence separating resilient demand from a narrow technology-led market.

## Skeptic conclusion

A modest H001 increase is defensible because the cleanest new cross-check—credit—remains tight and CPI is contained. The increase should remain small because CPI matched consensus rather than surprising positively, the recent oil shock has not yet been tested in consumer inflation, and labor weakness remains unresolved. H002 should lose some weight for lack of credit confirmation; H003 should lose only a little because its lagged energy/term-premium channel remains live.
