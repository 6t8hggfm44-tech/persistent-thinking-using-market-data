# Skeptic Report — 2026-08-10

**Pre-update leading hypothesis:** H002 Late-Cycle Recession, weight 0.32.

## Strongest case against H002

1. **Earnings are still strong.** Aggregate S&P 500 earnings evidence available Aug. 10 is inconsistent with a broad earnings contraction already underway.
2. **Risk assets remain resilient.** The S&P 500 finished only 0.1% below Friday's record despite a 5.1% WTI jump and higher long yields; that is not the broad risk-off confirmation H002 expects.
3. **Credit confirmation is absent, not positive.** The latest verified HY OAS input remains stale. H002 should not receive credit for an unobserved spread widening.
4. **One negative payroll print remains vulnerable to revision/noise.** Persistence has not yet been demonstrated.

## Hidden assumptions still carrying H002

- July payroll weakness is the start of a sequence rather than an isolated print.
- Strong current earnings are lagging rather than genuinely resilient.
- Credit stress will eventually validate the labor signal.
- Inflation/oil pressure will not become the dominant constraint first.

## Strongest alternative explanation

A mixed H001/H003 regime can explain the current observations without recession: labor is soft enough to reduce policy-tightening pressure, while oil/geopolitical risk and Treasury supply keep long yields elevated; strong earnings keep equities near records. This alternative is currently at least as plausible as H002.

## Highest-information discriminator

Fresh high-yield spread data plus the next labor and inflation observations. H002 should gain only if labor weakness persists and is joined by material credit widening and/or earnings deterioration. If inflation remains sticky and long yields stay high while credit and earnings remain resilient, H003 should gain instead. If inflation moderates and credit/earnings remain resilient, H001 should gain.
