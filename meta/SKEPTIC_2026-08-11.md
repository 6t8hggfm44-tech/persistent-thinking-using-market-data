# Skeptic Review — 2026-08-11

**Leading hypothesis attacked:** H001 Soft Landing (weight 0.31 before review)  
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-11T17:03:43-04:00

## Strongest contrary evidence

1. Oil rose again to roughly $83.2 WTI after the Aug. 10 surge, showing that the geopolitical commodity shock is persistent enough to threaten the disinflation path assumed by a clean soft-landing story.
2. Long yields remain historically restrictive near 4.7% even after easing on Aug. 11. A soft landing requires labor cooling without financial conditions tightening enough to damage earnings or credit; that condition is not yet secured.
3. The July labor contraction remains unresolved by one additional equity session. Strong current earnings are lagging evidence and could coexist temporarily with a developing slowdown.

## Hidden assumptions in H001

- That commodity-driven headline inflation does not materially reaccelerate core inflation or inflation expectations.
- That weakening labor is mostly normalization/noise rather than the start of persistent contraction.
- That credit remains contained; this remains under-observed because fresh verified HY OAS data is still missing.
- That current earnings resilience is sufficiently forward-looking to offset labor and rate risks.

## Alternative explanations for Aug. 11

- The modest equity decline may be positioning/geopolitical risk ahead of CPI rather than evidence for any macro regime.
- The fall in the 10-year yield while oil rose could reflect growth concerns, auction dynamics, or pre-CPI positioning rather than renewed disinflation confidence.
- Small-cap resilience is inconsistent with a simple broad recession-risk interpretation of the session.

## Highest-information discriminator

The Aug. 12 CPI release and cross-asset reaction should be more informative than Aug. 11 price action:
- benign CPI + lower long yields + stable/rising equities favors H001;
- hot CPI + higher long yields + equity weakness favors H003;
- benign CPI + worsening credit/growth-sensitive assets would preserve or strengthen H002.

## Skeptic conclusion

Do **not** raise H001 after P000010's correct direction. The forecast result is a scoring observation, not evidence that H001 is right. Conversely, do not cut H001 on one modest down day. Hold all hypothesis weights unchanged until the CPI discriminator or fresher credit evidence arrives.
