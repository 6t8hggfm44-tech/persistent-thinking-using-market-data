# Skeptic — 2026-08-19

**Leading hypothesis entering pass:** H001 Soft Landing (0.34)

## Strongest contrary case

H001 may be underestimating a policy-constraint squeeze. The July 28-29 FOMC minutes show that several participants were prepared to raise rates and many expected higher rates would be needed if inflation failed to return toward 2%. Meanwhile Brent/WTI reached their highest closes since July 24. Earlier evidence already showed weak housing and household-facing production. If the oil shock keeps inflation persistent while household demand remains soft, the Fed may have limited room to ease, allowing H003's high-rate regime to transmit into H002-style demand deterioration.

## Hidden assumptions in H001

1. Recent softer CPI/PPI and employment data will dominate the July FOMC's inflation concern rather than merely delay tightening.
2. The Treasury buyback announcement's long-end yield relief will persist enough to matter for financial conditions rather than reverse quickly.
3. Tight credit spreads and low layoffs will continue to offset weak housing/consumer categories.
4. Elevated oil will not create sufficiently durable inflation or purchasing-power pressure to overturn the benign landing.

## Alternative explanations for today's long-yield move

The Aug. 19 fall in 10- and 30-year CMT yields cannot be read as straightforward evidence of weaker inflation or stronger growth expectations. Treasury simultaneously announced larger long-end liquidity-support buybacks. Debt-management/liquidity policy, inflation expectations, growth expectations, issuance, private duration supply, and risk premia can all affect the same endpoint.

## Evidence against the attack

The FOMC minutes describe the July information set and predate subsequent softer inflation and labor data. Treasury's official curve fell from 4.71% to 4.65% at 10 years and from 5.28% to 5.19% at 30 years on Aug. 19, easing financial conditions at the margin. July aggregate industrial production remained positive, and no new credit-stress or layoff confirmation was available before this cutoff.

## Discriminators

- P000017: Aug. 20 initial claims >=220,000 directly tests whether household-side weakness is transmitting into layoffs.
- Persistence of oil near current levels plus later inflation expectations/price data tests whether H003's commodity channel survives beyond spot-price noise.
- Long-yield interpretation should increasingly rely on decomposition/joint evidence rather than the CMT endpoint alone because Treasury buyback policy is now a material confounder.

## Skeptic conclusion

Do not overturn H001. Reduce it modestly and raise H003 modestly: H001 0.33 / H002 0.25 / H003 0.30 / H004 0.12. The evidence is consistent with a tighter inflation-policy constraint but not sufficient to establish renewed tightening or recession.
