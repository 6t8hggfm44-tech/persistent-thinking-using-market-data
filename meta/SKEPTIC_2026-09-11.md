Falling equities can reflect discount rates, cash-flow concerns, or positioning. Neither endpoint by itself identifies H003.

Updating H003 aggressively before that higher-information test would risk substituting a vivid commodity shock for the precommitted discriminator.

## Hidden assumptions to resist

- That headline PPI and core CPI have the same causal content.
- That a supply-driven oil shock necessarily produces persistent core inflation rather than a temporary price-level shock plus weaker demand.
- That market-implied Fed probabilities are independent confirmation rather than downstream processing of the same public data.
- That one realized buyback operation can be evaluated from same-day long yields without isolating concurrent duration/inflation news.
- That repeated low initial claims validate all parts of the labor model.
