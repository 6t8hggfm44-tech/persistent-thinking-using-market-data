**Prior weight:** 0.25

## Thesis
Disinflation continues without a major contraction; earnings and financial conditions remain broadly supportive.

## Evidence expected if true
- Labor softens without persistent contraction.
- Credit spreads remain contained.
- Inflation moderates enough to permit easier policy.
- Earnings estimates remain resilient.

## Evidence surprising if true
- Sustained payroll contraction plus widening credit spreads.
- Broad earnings downgrades and deteriorating market breadth.
- Inflation reacceleration sufficient to force tighter policy.

## Distinguishing prediction
Weak labor data accompanied by stable credit and resilient earnings should favor H001 over H002.
