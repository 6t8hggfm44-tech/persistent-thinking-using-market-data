# H004: Productivity Boom

**Prior weight:** 0.25  
**Current weight:** 0.12  
**Status:** Active competing hypothesis

## Thesis
Productivity gains raise real growth and earnings capacity enough to offset part of the rate and valuation constraint.

## Evidence expected if true
- Strong earnings growth without equivalent inflation acceleration.
- Improving margins/productivity alongside resilient output.
- Technology leadership broadens into productivity beneficiaries.

## Evidence surprising if true
- Weak productivity, falling margins, and broad earnings downgrades.
- Growth requires increasing inflation pressure rather than efficiency gains.

## Distinguishing prediction
Earnings and real output should remain stronger than implied by labor inputs alone if H004 is important.

The simultaneous rise in financing concessions can even become a constraint on the buildout. Strong services activity likewise does not isolate productivity from nominal demand.
