# H002: Late-Cycle Recession

**Prior weight:** 0.25  
**Current weight:** 0.25  
**Status:** Active competing hypothesis

## Thesis
Restrictive financial conditions and weakening labor/growth eventually produce a material earnings and risk-asset downturn.

## Evidence expected if true
- Repeated labor weakness and negative revisions.
- Credit-spread widening.
- Earnings downgrades and deteriorating breadth.
- Defensive leadership and tighter financial conditions.

## Evidence surprising if true
- Persistent labor weakness with tight credit and resilient earnings.
- Broad risk-asset participation despite worsening macro data.

## Distinguishing prediction
If labor weakness persists over 1–3 months and credit spreads materially widen while breadth deteriorates, H002 should gain weight over H001.
