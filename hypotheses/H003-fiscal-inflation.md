# H003: Fiscal / Inflation Regime

**Prior weight:** 0.25  
**Current weight:** 0.30  
**Status:** Active competing hypothesis

## Thesis
Persistent fiscal impulse and issuance pressure keep nominal growth, inflation risk, and long yields structurally elevated.

## Evidence expected if true
- Sticky inflation despite softer labor.
- Elevated term premium / long yields.
- Continued issuance pressure and rate-sensitive valuation constraint.
- Commodity shocks transmit strongly into inflation expectations.

## Evidence surprising if true
- Durable disinflation with falling long yields despite strong nominal activity.
- Fiscal expansion without observable rate or inflation pressure.

## Distinguishing prediction
Inflation surprises should transmit more strongly into long yields and equity valuation under H003 than under H001.

## Update history
- Baseline prior 0.25.
- 2026-08-08 posterior 0.27.
- 2026-08-10 posterior 0.30: WTI rose 5.1% and the 10-year yield moved to roughly 4.70% despite weak labor data, while Treasury issuance remained salient. This strengthens the state-dependent inflation/term-premium channel but does not yet establish persistent inflation.
